DeepChopper is a genomic language model built on HyenaDNA that identifies and removes adapter-bridged chimera artifacts in nanopore direct RNA sequencing data. It is described in the paper *Genomic Language Model Mitigates Chimera Artifacts in Nanopore Direct RNA Sequencing*.
